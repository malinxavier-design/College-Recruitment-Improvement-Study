## Overview
This project analyzes survey responses from non-computer science majors at County College of Morris (CCM) collected during Fall 2024. The primary objective is to evaluate enrollment motivations, measure pre-college outreach exposure, assess interest in subsequent computing courses, and identify high-demand computing topics to deliver actionable, data-driven recruitment and messaging strategies for the CCM IT Department.

## Key Research Questions & Findings

### 1. Primary Recruitment Motivations (Instructor Assigned)
* **Finding:** Degree requirement fulfillment, personal enrichment, and career advancement serve as the primary drivers behind non-major enrollment in computing classes.
* **Recruitment Strategy:** Position promotional campaigns around career versatility and skill amplification, highlighting how introductory computing knowledge complements non-CS degree paths (e.g., Business, Humanities, Fine Arts).

### 2. Interest in Subsequent Computing Courses
* **Finding:** Survey data demonstrates strong latent interest among non-majors in continuing computing coursework past their initial class.
* **Recruitment Strategy:** Send targeted registration reminders and elective highlights (via Canvas announcements or direct email) to currently enrolled non-majors right before registration windows open.

### 3. Pre-College Outreach Engagement
* **Finding:** Campus-wide outreach events—such as general Open Houses and High School Information Sessions—yield significantly higher student participation than specialized niche programs.
* **Recruitment Strategy:** Concentrate departmental outreach resources on high-volume general college events by featuring interactive IT demonstrations and student showcases.

### 4. High-Demand Computing Topics
* **Finding:** Web Development, Computer Programming, and Data Analytics command the highest student interest among non-majors, outperforming infrastructure and hardware repair topics.
* **Recruitment Strategy:** Highlight practical Web Development and introductory programming modules in marketing collateral aimed at non-CS majors to maximize registration volume.

---

## Actionable Recommendations for CCM IT Department
1. **Capitalize on General Admissions Events:** Integrate IT program showcases directly into campus-wide Open Houses and High School Visit Days where prospective student touchpoints are highest.
2. **Emphasize Applied Skillsets:** Focus messaging on Web Development, Python, and Data Analytics, stressing their immediate utility across non-tech career fields.
3. **Engage Current Non-Major Enrollees:** Retarget non-majors currently taking introductory computing courses near registration dates to encourage elective pathway continuation.
